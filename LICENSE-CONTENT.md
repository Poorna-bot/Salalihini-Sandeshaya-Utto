## What this covers

- `src/data/poem.json` — the 111 Elu Sinhala verses of *Salalihini Sandeshaya*
- `src/data/meanings-1.ts` through `src/data/meanings-4.ts` — the modern Sinhala and English explanations and context notes
- `src/data/sections.ts` — section descriptions
- Cultural and historical prose on `/about` and `/map`
