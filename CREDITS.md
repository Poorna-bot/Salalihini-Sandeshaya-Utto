## Poem Text

- **සැළලිහිණි සන්දේශය (*Salalihini Sandeshaya*)** by **Thotagamuwe Sri Rahula Thera** (composed c. 1450 CE in Jayawardhanapura Kotte).
- Digital Sinhala text retrieved from **[Sinhala Wikibooks: සැළලිහිණි සන්දේශය](https://si.wikibooks.org/wiki/%E0%B7%83%E0%B7%90%E0%B7%85%E0%B6%BD%E0%B7%92%E0%B7%84%E0%B7%92%E0%B6%AB%E0%B7%92_%E0%B7%83%E0%B6%B1%E0%B7%8A%E0%B6%AF%E0%B7%9A%E0%B7%81%E0%B6%BA)**, originally contributed from the **e-Pothgula** digital library (`http://epothgula.com/selalihiniya`), licensed under [Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International (CC BY-SA 4.0)](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/).

## Historical & Literary Background Reading

Used for factual verification only (all Sinhala and English explanations on this site are written fresh in plain words):

- Dharshani Lakmali Jayasinghe, **[Translating Worlds through Words: The Bird-View of the Selalihini Sandeshaya (The Starling's Message)](https://shc.stanford.edu/arcade/publications/dibur/curated/translating-worlds-through-words-bird-view-selalihini-sandeshaya)**, *DIBUR / Arcade*, Stanford Humanities Center (2022).
- Dharshani Lakmali Jayasinghe, **[A translation of select quatrains from the fifteenth-century Sinhala poem Selalihini Sandeshaya](https://shc.stanford.edu/arcade/publications/dibur/curated/translation-select-quatrains-fifteenth-century-sinhala-poem)**, *DIBUR / Arcade*, Stanford Humanities Center (2022).
- **[Thotagamuwe Sri Rahula Thera](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thotagamuwe_Sri_Rahula_Thera)** — Wikipedia, for biographical context on the Vijayabahu Pirivena at Totagamuwa and the poet's works.
- **[Kingdom of Kotte](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kingdom_of_Kotte)** and **[Parakramabahu VI](https://de.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parakramabahu_VI.)** — Wikipedia, for historical context on Jayawardhanapura Kotte, the Tooth Relic shrine, Prince Sapumal's Jaffna campaign, and Kelaniya Raja Maha Vihara.
- **[Some aspects of Selalihini Sandeshaya](http://archives.dailynews.lk/2004/02/04/artscop13.html)** — *Daily News* (Sri Lanka) archives, on the fifteen stanzas portraying God Vibhishana.
