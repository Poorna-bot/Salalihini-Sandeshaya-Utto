# Support & Questions

Need help using or running **සැළලිහිණි සංදේශය · The Starling's Message**?

- **Spotted an error in a verse or explanation?** Open a [Content Correction issue](https://github.com/Poorna-bot/salalihini-sandeshaya/issues/new?template=content_correction.yml).
- **Found a rendering or browser bug?** Open a [Bug Report](https://github.com/Poorna-bot/salalihini-sandeshaya/issues/new?template=bug_report.yml) and include your device, browser, and whether WebGL or Calm Mode was active.
- **Have an idea or improvement?** Open a [Feature Request](https://github.com/Poorna-bot/salalihini-sandeshaya/issues/new?template=feature_request.yml).
- **Building locally?** Check the [Quick start](./README.md#quick-start) in `README.md` and [`CONTRIBUTING.md`](./CONTRIBUTING.md).
